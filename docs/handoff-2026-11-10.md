## Where things stand

**Goal (unchanged):** one image flashable to eMMC in one shot with working
drivers (USB hub, Ethernet). Own DDR init stays paused until then.

**The eth/USB problem is not firmware.** Both hang off the carrier rail
`VCC3V3_SYS` (GL3523 hub, RTL8211F PHY, RTC, fusb302, HDMI DDC), enabled by
`PMIC_EXT_EN_OUT` from the RK806 on the SoM. Evidence collected today:

- Same firmware chain, 2026-11-09: rail **ON** 09:44-15:26 (`serial-logs/
  2026-11-09-1145-no-pcie3.log`: `RealtekPhyInit: Found Realtek RTL8211F`;
  bt0 probe all 1s), **OFF** before 07:38 and again at 19:01 (Armbian run,
  only root hubs). The 11-09 docs' "off, unexplained vs Android" framing was
  wrong; a correction is appended to `docs/yy3588-firmware-status-2026-11-09.md`.
- Stock Android 14 DTB (extracted from `yy3588_android14.0_*-001.img`,
  `rk3588-yyt-lp4-v10`) has no rail enable either. Only extra GPIO regulator
  is `vcc_camera1` on GPIO1_B2 = camera header. Dead end.
- Dave's observations: board lights stay on when powered off with serial
  attached (adapter TX back-feeds the RX pad; no VCC wire in use); serial
  USB attaches/detaches on the workstation when OTG is also connected
  (ground loop); MaskROM sometimes not entered. Prime suspect for the
  session-to-session rail state: pre-biased 3.3 V domain at power-on from the
  back-feed, plus the ground loop.
- No multimeter. Parts ordered: DSD TECH SH-G01A ADuM3160 USB isolator (goes
  between workstation and serial adapter only, never on OTG), JST PH 2.0 mm
  4-pin to Dupont leads for J43 (the yellow 4-pin wafer; pin 1 GND, 2 board
  TX, 3 board RX, 4 = VCC3V3_SYS, leave unconnected).

## 2026-11-10 ~19:30: 3.3 V rail measured with a Luckfox Pico Max

No meter, so: Pico Max (factory firmware) GPIO1_C7
(pull-down, Schmitt input) read via `io -4 -r 0xff530070` bit 23, with 1N4007
diodes in series as a ladder. Calibration on the Pico's own 3V3: 3 diodes = 1,
4 diodes = 0. YY3588 J43 leftmost pin in the "rail present" Debian boot:
direct = 1, 3 diodes = 1, 4 diodes = 0. **So VCC3V3_SYS is at about 3.3 V in
that state** (earlier 0 readings were a miswired breadboard; ignore them).
The Pico's ADC pins are 1.8 V only and saturate; do not put the rail on them.

So with the rail genuinely at 3.3 V: RTC, fusb302, MDIO and PHY CLKOUT125 work,
but the RTL8211F has no link with a cable in and no RXC, neither USB hub
(GL3523, FE1.1s) attaches in EDK2 or Linux, and both PCIe 2.0 ports fail to
link. Rail level is not the explanation for those. The "U2 NB680 damaged"
guess above is not supported for the present state; the rail is still absent
on most cold starts (5 of 6 at 17:31). Unchecked: VCC5V0_SYS level, carrier
VCC1V8_SYS (U3), USB-A VBUS.

ON_SOURCE 0x74: [7] ON_PWRON, [6] ON_VDC, [5] ON_ABNORMAL, [4] RESTART_RESETB,
[3] RESTART_PWRON_LP, [2] RESTART_PWRCTRL, [1] RESTART_DEV_RST, [0] RESTART_WDT.
OFF_SOURCE 0x75: [7] OFF_PWRCTRL, [6] VB_SYS_OV, [5] OFF_TSD, [4] OFF_SYNC,
[3] OFF_DEV_OFF, [2] OFF_PWRON_LP, [1] OFF_ABNORMAL, [0] OFF_VB_LO.
(The vendor kernel's rk806.h field order does not match this table.)

- Loop restarts: off_src 0x02 = OFF_ABNORMAL, on_src 0x20 = ON_ABNORMAL
  (0x30 adds RESTART_RESETB). Datasheet 4.1.3 item 7: with 0x5F[7]=0 the PMIC
  turns off at once when VCCA/VCC1/VCC2 (the SoM's 4.0 V VCC_SYSIN) goes
  below VB_UV (typ 2.7 V) or above VB_OV (typ 6.0 V), then restarts.
- All 10 loop restarts logged today happen at the same point: right after
  systemd-boot opens `\Image` (43 MB eMMC read), and only in the
  5 V-up / 3.3 V-down state. First power-on is always 0x40 / 0x00.
- 11-09 logs: 8 x (0x40, 0x00), 1 x (0x30, 0x02) in `0510-probe2`.
- Reading: the SoM input rail is collapsing under a load step, i.e. the
  12 V feed (supply, cable, jack) or the 4 V buck U23 MP8759 is marginal.
  Not yet told apart from an overvoltage spike. Datasheet copy:
  scratchpad only (rockchip.fr "RK806 datasheet V1.4.pdf").
- Next: strip loads (keyboard, HDMI, Ethernet) and retest; supply rated
  >= 12 V 3 A; bt0 could print 0x5D-0x61 and INT_STS 0x77/0x79 to tell UV
